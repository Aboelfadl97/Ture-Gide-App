# Cairo

Cairo is the capital city of Egypt.

Cairo is the largest city in Egypt and one of the largest cities in Africa.

The city is home to many famous attractions including:

- The Egyptian Museum
- Cairo Tower
- Khan El Khalili Bazaar
- Al-Azhar Mosque

Cairo is connected to Giza where the Great Pyramids are located.

The Cairo Metro is one of the fastest public transportation systems in the Middle East.
